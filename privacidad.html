1. Roles: who is responsible for your data

  • Data processed on behalf of restaurants (“Customer Data”). The restaurant that uses the system is the controller of the data of its staff, guests and suppliers. The Provider acts as processor, strictly to provide the service, under the Data Processing Addendum in the Terms of Service (Annex 1).
  • Data processed by the Provider for its own purposes. The Provider is the controller of a limited set of data: manager/owner contact details, records of acceptance of the Terms (who, when, which version), and support communications. Legal bases: performance of the contract and legitimate interest in operating and securing the service.

2. What data the platform processes

  • Staff: name, username, e-mail, role(s) and permissions; work schedules; clock-in/out records and declared hours; wage rates, payroll summaries and employee charges; profile photographs; internal messages, posts, documents and task records.
  • Guests / customers of the restaurant: reservation details (name, contact, party size, times), customer records the restaurant creates.
  • Business records: sales, orders, cash register operations, invoices (uploaded files), inventory and supplier data — which may include names of natural persons.
  • Technical: authentication tokens, terminal identifiers, and server logs needed to operate and secure the service. No advertising trackers and no third-party analytics are used.

3. Where the data lives — sub-processors

The service runs on the following infrastructure providers (sub-processors under Art. 28 GDPR). Transfers outside the EU/EEA are covered by an adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, “DPF”) and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (“SCC”).

4. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all clients.
  • Individual accounts with hashed passwords; role- and permission-based access control (e.g. wages visible only to authorised roles).
  • Each restaurant's data isolated in a dedicated database schema (multi-tenant isolation).
  • Regular database backups; breach notification to the affected restaurant without undue delay.

5. Retention and deletion

Customer Data is kept while the restaurant's subscription is active. After termination the restaurant may request an export within 30 days; after 60 days data may be deleted from production systems, with backup copies expiring in the normal rotation. The Provider's own records (contract, acceptance, invoicing) are kept as long as required by law.

6. Your rights

Data subjects have the GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. If you are an employee or guest of a restaurant using this system, please address your request to that restaurant (the controller); the Provider assists the restaurant in fulfilling it. For data the Provider controls (clause 1), write to figueroa.lucasn@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g. IMY in Sweden, Datatilsynet in Denmark).

ℹ️ Notice for restaurant staff. If your employer uses this system: it records your work schedules, clock-ins/outs and declared hours (for planning and payroll), your wage data (visible only to authorised managers), your profile photo if uploaded, and the messages, posts, documents and task confirmations you create in the internal tools. Your employer decides what is collected and why, and is your point of contact for questions and for exercising your data protection rights.

7. Cookies and local storage

The web panel stores a session token and interface preferences in your browser's local storage. No advertising cookies or cross-site trackers are used.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the version and date above identify the current text. Material changes will be notified in the product. Contact: figueroa.lucasn@gmail.com.

  • Técnicos: tokens de sesión, identificadores de terminal y logs necesarios para operar y asegurar el servicio. No se usan rastreadores publicitarios ni analítica de terceros.

4. Seguridad

  • Cifrado en tránsito (HTTPS/TLS) en todos los clientes.
  • Cuentas individuales con contraseñas con hash; control de acceso por roles y permisos (p. ej. los salarios solo los ven los roles autorizados).
  • Datos de cada restaurante aislados en un esquema de base de datos dedicado (aislamiento multi-inquilino).
  • Copias de seguridad periódicas; notificación de violaciones de seguridad al restaurante afectado sin dilación indebida.

5. Conservación y borrado

Los Datos del Cliente se conservan mientras la suscripción del restaurante esté activa. Tras la terminación, el restaurante puede pedir una exportación en 30 días; a los 60 días los datos pueden eliminarse de producción, y las copias de backup expiran en la rotación normal. Los registros propios del Proveedor (contrato, aceptaciones, facturación) se conservan lo que exija la ley.

6. Tus derechos

Los interesados tienen los derechos RGPD de acceso, rectificación, supresión, limitación, portabilidad y oposición. Si sos empleado o comensal de un restaurante que usa este sistema, dirigí tu solicitud a ese restaurante (el responsable); el Proveedor le asiste para atenderla. Para los datos de los que el Proveedor es responsable (cláusula 1), escribí a figueroa.lucasn@gmail.com. También podés reclamar ante tu autoridad de protección de datos (IMY en Suecia, Datatilsynet en Dinamarca).

ℹ️ Aviso para el personal de los restaurantes. Si tu empleador usa este sistema: registra tus horarios, fichajes y horas declaradas (para planificar y liquidar la nómina), tus datos salariales (visibles solo para los gerentes autorizados), tu foto de perfil si se sube, y los mensajes, publicaciones, documentos y confirmaciones de tareas que creás en las herramientas internas. Tu empleador decide qué se recoge y para qué, y es tu punto de contacto para preguntas y para ejercer tus derechos.

7. Cookies y almacenamiento local

El panel web guarda un token de sesión y preferencias de interfaz en el almacenamiento local del navegador. No se usan cookies publicitarias ni rastreadores entre sitios.

8. Cambios y contacto

Podemos actualizar esta política; la versión y fecha del encabezado identifican el texto vigente. Los cambios materiales se avisarán en el producto. Contacto: figueroa.lucasn@gmail.com.
